Case studies

  • 01 · Shipped A decision tree for out-of-network reviews Our out-of-network reviews depended on rules passed along by word of mouth and checks across several systems. I worked with leadership, compliance, appeals, and the authorization department to turn them into one written decision tree.
  • 02 · Shipped Fixing a workaround in expedited reviews Reviewers had started deciding expedited requests the same day to avoid sending a denial letter, and providers learned to mark routine requests as expedited. After we changed the workflow, staff noticed about 90% fewer unnecessary expedited requests.
  • 03 · Proposal Teaching new nurses to troubleshoot implanted ports New nurses on an oncology unit were passing their skills check-off but still getting stuck when a port didn't work as expected. I looked into why and proposed a simulation-based training to close the gap.

What's next

  • Recommendation Moving prior authorization requests off fax Late authorizations kept arriving in waves because nobody could see the fax backlog building. I recommended moving providers to the portal and wrote a plan for measuring the change.
  • Concept AI-assisted clinical review A concept for what a model could prepare before a nurse opens a case, and which decisions should always stay with the nurse.

Other work
