AI-assisted clinical review

I'm working on a product concept for AI-assisted utilization management review, built on the workflows in my out-of-network decision path and my prior auth intake recommendation. This page is in progress and will be updated when the concept is ready.

What it will cover

  • What a model drafts. Which parts of a review, such as network status lookups, distance checks and pulling the relevant documentation, could be prepared before a nurse opens the case.
  • Where a nurse must decide. Which determinations need clinical judgment and a human sign-off, and how the tool makes that clear.
  • How it fails. The ways a model could get a review wrong, and how the workflow catches them.
  • How to measure it. Accuracy, turnaround and safety, including overturn rates on appeal.
